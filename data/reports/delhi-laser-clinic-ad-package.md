## 2. Ad Formats — 5 Proven Reel Templates

### Format A: "The Process Reveal" (Best for Laser Hair Removal)
**Why it works:** People fear the unknown. Showing the actual procedure demystifies it.

**Structure (25-30s):**
| Time | Visual | Audio/Text |
|------|--------|------------|
| 0-2s | Close-up of laser device powering on | HOOK: "This is what painless laser hair removal actually looks like" |
| 2-8s | Doctor prepping patient, applying gel | "Step 1: We apply a cooling gel..." |
| 8-18s | Actual laser pulses on skin (close-up) | "The laser targets only the hair follicle..." |
| 18-25s | Patient smiling, wiping area | "And that's it. No burns, no redness." |
| 25-30s | Doctor to camera | CTA: "Book your trial session — link in bio" |

**On-screen text overlays:**
- "POV: Your first laser session at Delhi Laser Clinic"
- Price tag: "Starting ₹999/session" (if applicable)

### Format B: "Myth vs Truth" (Best for Pigmentation/Acne)
**Why it works:** Correcting misinformation positions the doctor as the authority.

**Structure (20-25s):**
| Time | Visual | Audio/Text |
|------|--------|------------|
| 0-2s | Doctor looking shocked/disappointed | HOOK: "Stop putting lemon on your face!" |
| 2-8s | Split screen: wrong method vs right method | "MYTH: Home remedies fix pigmentation" |
| 8-15s | Doctor explains to camera | "TRUTH: Pigmentation is deeper than your skin surface..." |
| 15-20s | Quick montage of clinic treatments | "Professional treatments target melanin at the source" |
| 20-25s | Doctor CTA | "DM 'GLOW' for a free skin assessment" |

**Suggested myths to bust (series of 10+):**
1. "Lemon juice removes dark spots" — pigmentation depth
2. "Laser hair removal causes more hair" — growth cycle science
3. "Acne means dirty skin" — hormonal vs bacterial
4. "Anti-aging starts at 40" — collagen loss at 25
5. "Expensive creams = better results" — active ingredients matter

2. **Audio:** Lapel mic on doctor (₹500-1000 on Amazon) — clinic AC noise kills audio
3. **Language:** Hinglish (60% Hindi, 40% English) — matches Delhi audience
4. **Hashtags per post (15-20):**
